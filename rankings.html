Our GPU rankings and CPU rankings for 2026 are based on a blend of real-world gaming performance, ray tracing and upscaling features, power efficiency, and price-to-performance value. What to Look for in a GPU or CPU

For a gaming PC, the most important spec is usually the GPU: look at VRAM, memory bus width, boost clock, and ray tracing support. For a CPU, consider core count, threads, boost clock, TDP for your cooler, and the motherboard socket compatibility.
